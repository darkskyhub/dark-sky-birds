## What the live build holds

- Two regions: Warrumbungle Mountains and Pilliga Forest.
- 168 species in the catalogue.
- Warrumbungle abundance listed on 163 cards.
- Pilliga abundance listed on 163 cards.
- The landing cards still say "163 species in this region". That number is stale. Please treat the catalogue count above as the check figure.

## Please check, in this order

1. Landing line reads: Choose a region, then browse by how common, what kind of bird, then A to Z. No "field guide" sentence. No Dark Sky Eco Retreat.
2. Join Warrumbungle Mountains. Open Waterbirds. Open Australasian Darter. Android back arrow returns to Waterbirds, not All groups.
3. On I saw this, Location drop-down includes Other. Other opens a typed field (try Pilliga Pottery).
4. On Name this, phone shows three buttons: Take a photo, Select a photo, Record a call. Select a photo must open the gallery. Desktop may keep a file picker.
5. Heading is More Information, not Ask Grok. Button is Submit, not Send to Grok.
6. Submit opener starts: I am a visitor in the dark sky region of Warrumbungle Shire, New South Wales, near Coonabarabran.
7. After a report, footer line: This APP and the information above was proudly created with Grok
8. After a named identification, the app asks before storing a photo and details.
9. After an unidentified bird, the app adds the record, congratulates you, then asks the same consent questions.
10. Name is the only mandatory field, and only if you said yes to recording details.

## Please do not treat as a public launch

This is a tester pass. Crystal Kingdom is the town desk later. National Parks and Wildlife Service (NPWS) Discovery Centres own the trails. Do not post the grok.me address on Facebook as the public home. Use the GitHub Pages address when you need a stable link.
