## What the live build holds

- Two regions: Warrumbungle Mountains and Pilliga Forest.
- 168 species in the catalogue.
- Each region card still says 163 species in this region. That number is the abundance-listed count. Treat 168 as the unique-species check figure.
- Bottom tabs: Birds, Identify, My List.
- My List and My Notes stay on the phone. No login.

## Please check, in this order

1. Landing line reads: Choose a region, then browse by how common, what kind of bird, then A to Z. No field guide sentence. No Dark Sky Eco Retreat.
2. Join Warrumbungle Mountains. Open Waterbirds. Open Australasian Darter. Android back arrow returns to Waterbirds, not All groups.
3. Bottom tab My List, not I saw this. Empty list says kept on this phone.
4. Identify heading is Help me identify this bird, not Help me name this.
5. Instruction line: Add a photo, a call recording as well as a good description in the My Notes section then press Submit.
6. Three buttons: Take a photo, Select a photo, Record a call. My Notes box sits under them. Submit is on the same screen.
7. More Information and Ask Grok are gone. There is no visible prompt box.
8. Submit stays on the Identify screen with a wait icon. Do not expect a new page.
9. No service or no name: No identification yet. Try again later when you have service, or add a clearer photo or a better description.
10. Named and in the catalogue: panel with the name, then Accept identification (opens the bird card) and Search again (returns to Identify with photo and notes kept).
11. Named and not in the catalogue: This bird is not in the catalogue. Saves to My List. Notify Dark Sky Hub opens mail to darkskyhubnsw@gmail.com if the phone allows it.
12. On a saved My List bird you can edit My Notes and add a photo from camera or gallery.
